About this policy

The Sora Games provides Slitherloop: Snake Arena for Android (com.slitherloop.snakearena). This policy explains information handled by the game and its integrated services. You can play the offline game without enabling optional Firebase analytics or crash-report uploads. Ads require an internet connection. The current game has no player account or online multiplayer service.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your settings, personal best and age-group/privacy choices locally. The age choice is a broad group, not your date of birth. Clearing app storage or uninstalling can remove these local records; Android backup or restore settings may affect whether they return. Starting a new round does not erase your saved personal best or privacy choice.

Optional analytics and crash reports

For eligible adults, Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics help us understand gameplay, ad reliability, performance and crashes. Information can include round starts and endings, scores, survival time, boost/revive use, ad requests, loads, displays, rewards and errors, performance samples, app and Unity versions, Android version, device model and memory characteristics. Firebase may also handle installation/app-instance identifiers, timestamps, diagnostic stack traces and approximate country/region inferred from an internet connection. The app does not add player names, a custom user ID, precise GPS location or advertising identifiers to its custom Firebase events. The Firebase SDK's own automatic events and diagnostic handling also apply when enabled.

Your analytics choices

Analytics and crash uploads are off for under-18 and unknown-age players. For adults, the current app requests opt-in outside the United States and when the country is unknown. In the United States it presents a notice with an option to turn collection off. An explicit saved choice is remembered. You can review or withdraw your choice from main-menu Settings > Privacy choices. Refusing does not prevent offline gameplay. The app does not send a custom refusal event to Firebase. Withdrawing stops future optional collection and resets local Analytics data, but does not automatically erase reports already received by providers. Crashlytics may retain a local crash cache while uploading is disabled and may send it if reporting is later enabled.

Automatic country lookup

To choose the initial regional privacy flow, the app makes an HTTPS request to api.country.is. This service receives your IP address and normal connection information and returns a country code. The game does not request GPS access or ask this service for your street address or state. A failed lookup uses the more restrictive opt-in flow. This lookup is separate from optional Firebase analytics; the provider controls its own service logs.

Advertising

We use Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) mediation with integrated Unity Ads and Google AdMob services to provide rewarded videos and interstitials. Availability depends on network configuration and fill. Rewarded videos are optional, including a revive opportunity. Interstitials may appear at eligible round endings. Ad services can process IP address, device/app information, identifiers permitted by the platform and privacy configuration, ad interactions, diagnostic information and fraud-prevention signals, and infer approximate location. The app currently requests restricted/child-directed treatment, disables personalized-ad consent and requests device-ID opt-out through supported SDK settings. These flags do not mean that all ad-related network processing stops. Selecting Firebase analytics off does not disable ads or their operational processing.

Children and other players under 18

The game uses a broad age-group choice and keeps optional Firebase analytics and crash uploads off for under-18 and unknown-age players. Ads are requested with restricted settings for all players in this version. We do not ask children for names, email addresses or dates of birth to play. A parent or guardian can contact us about privacy concerns. Third-party processing is described above and in the providers' policies.

Why information is used and shared

Information is used to operate ads and rewards, maintain settings, understand and improve the game, investigate crashes and ad failures, measure performance, prevent abuse and meet legal obligations. It is handled by the service providers described here rather than published as player profiles. Providers may process information in countries other than yours and apply their own safeguards and retention rules. We do not sell a player contact list; sharing for advertising is described above and may be treated differently under regional privacy laws.

Retention, security and your rights

Local settings remain until cleared or changed. Data received by Firebase and ad providers follows the retention settings and policies of those services; this policy does not promise immediate or automatic deletion of every server record. We use HTTPS for the app's country lookup and the providers' supported SDK connections, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Depending on your country, you may have rights to access, correction, deletion, objection, restriction or withdrawal of consent, and to contact a local supervisory authority. Email us to make a request. We may need enough information to identify the relevant records and may be unable to associate a report with you because the game has no named player account. Do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.
